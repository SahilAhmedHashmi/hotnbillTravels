## Motion principles synthesized

- Cinematic: spend motion on a few coordinated scenes; use depth and pacing without obstructing navigation.
- Editorial: typography and crop lines move with short, controlled timing.
- Earthy: sine-based easing and slight asymmetry avoid mechanical repetition.
- Minimal: near-zero displacement; feedback communicates state rather than spectacle.
- Photographic: masks, crop continuity, and scale sequencing make images the spatial narrative.

Shared infrastructure is limited to reduced-motion detection, lifecycle cleanup, and animation scoping. Choreography, easing, displacement, masks, sequencing, and scroll triggers are version-specific.
