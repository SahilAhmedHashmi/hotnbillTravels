# Five design directions

Research sources are treated as principle references, not templates. Notes distinguish observation, inference, and implementation.

Reference index: [SiteInspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/) for cross-industry typographic, grid, minimal, and photographic classification; [Staufer & Hasler case study](https://sandrastaufer.com/design/post-500/) for responsive minimalist architecture and simplified journeys; [BGP relaunch case study](https://www.henkelhiedl.com/en/projects/bgp-bob-gysin-and-partner-relaunch-art-in-construction) for systematic separation of text and imagery; [Photo Elysée project](https://blocco.studio/photo-elysee) for photography-first bold minimal framing. These informed principles only; no layout, identity, copy, or asset was reproduced.

## 01 Cinematic

**Observation:** Immersive narrative work concentrates attention with a small number of large visual transitions and deliberate camera-like framing. It loses usability when navigation or reading pace is subordinated to spectacle.

**Inference:** Hornbill needs one opening landscape with a low visual horizon, restrained copy, and two equally legible commercial paths.

**Implementation:** Full-viewport Tawang frame; condensed dramatic scale; amber accent; long background settle; staggered destination rhythm. Mobile abandons parallax and uses a stable poster composition. Motion: slow dissolve and scale, with reduced-motion opt-out.

## 02 Luxury / Editorial

**Observation:** Strong editorial hospitality and fashion sites obtain luxury from typographic confidence, asymmetry, fine rules, and generous pacing—not gold ornament.

**Inference:** Travel planning can feel authored when company context precedes the catalogue and imagery behaves like a magazine plate.

**Implementation:** Paper-toned split hero, Majuli image plate, serif-led hierarchy, oxblood accent, sharp cards, and long section intervals. Motion is short and precise. Dark mode becomes an ink-and-parchment night edition.

## 03 Earthy / Natural

**Observation:** Effective nature-led brands abstract environmental qualities through material colour, contour, and rhythm. Literal leaf icons and recycled-paper decoration quickly become cliché.

**Inference:** Northeast India can be expressed through water, vegetation, mineral warmth, and road topography without adopting an “eco template.”

**Implementation:** Waterfall opening, forest/mineral palette, a terrain-like lower hero edge, gently arched image containers, and trust placed early. Motion is soft and elastic but never continuous.

## 04 Minimalist

**Observation:** Swiss and architecture references use disciplined grids, assertive sans typography, monochrome imagery, and strict separation of text and image. Interaction is convincing when it reinforces order.

**Inference:** The clearest expression of the dual offer is an almost diagrammatic hero: proposition at left, route image at right.

**Implementation:** Near-white field, blue functional accent, cropped monochrome Sela Pass plate, square controls, rule-based cards, no decorative transform on hover. Dark mode is graphite rather than inverted white.

## 05 Photographic

**Observation:** Photo essays create meaning through sequence, crop, scale changes, and captions. Portfolio conventions fail travel commerce when the path to inquiry disappears.

**Inference:** Photography can be the navigation layer while booking actions remain anchored in the opening frame and repeated after commercial context.

**Implementation:** Full-frame Dawki image, contact-sheet destination grid, extra-wide title, orange indexing accent, and denser image rhythm. Mobile converts the composition into a single stable visual sequence.

## Shared restraint and anti-slop rules

- No glass cards, gradient type, fake metrics, fake testimonials, awards, ratings, or generated landmarks.
- No component library was imported merely for visual novelty; the existing bespoke component system is stronger for coherence.
- Light is the default. Both themes retain direction-specific palette intent.
- Every direction keeps Book a Vehicle and Plan a Complete Trip visible at the first decision point.
- Nagaland and Manipur remain non-bookable geographic context only.
