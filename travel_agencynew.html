Who We Are

Born from the valleys of Assam, raised by the Northeast

We are a homegrown travel agency rooted in the rich, layered culture of Northeast India. For over a decade, we have been quietly crafting journeys that take travellers deep into the living world of Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Tripura, and Sikkim.

From the world's largest river island to the world's wettest place, from one-horned rhinos to hornbill festivals — we connect you to places that still feel like secrets.
