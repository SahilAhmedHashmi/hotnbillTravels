# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Travelers considering a journey through Northeast India who need to understand the region, choose between a locally driven vehicle and a complete planned trip, and make a credible enquiry with confidence. Mobile visitors are a first-class audience, not a reduced desktop case.

## Product Purpose

Hornbill Journeys helps visitors explore and enquire about travel across Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura, and Sikkim. Success means that a visitor understands what the region feels like, trusts the practical travel proposition, and can confidently choose either a vehicle with an experienced local driver or a complete journey.

## Positioning

Hornbill Journeys combines the practical confidence of well-maintained vehicles and experienced local drivers with complete, place-led journeys through Northeast India. It is neither a generic car-rental marketplace nor a generic destination-inspiration site.

## Operating Context

Visitors explore destinations, experiences, vehicles, and complete-trip ideas before continuing into the existing destination, fleet, experience, package, or enquiry routes. The current Photographic landing page is an experimental brand direction under review; it must not yet redefine the visual design of secondary routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offered destinations are limited to Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura, and Sikkim. Nagaland and Manipur must not be presented as offered destinations.
- The service proposition includes vehicle bookings with experienced local drivers, complete travel planning, individual experiences, and destination exploration.
- Existing routes, project data, enquiries, navigation, themes, and secondary-page functionality must remain intact.
- Light mode is the default; visitors can manually select dark mode.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, remain performant, and never be required to understand or operate the page.
- Prices, specifications, awards, reviews, statistics, guarantees, and package inclusions must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Hornbill Journeys.
- The brand balances premium craft with adventure, while remaining human, geographically authentic, contemporary, and useful.
- Northeast India must be presented as a diverse region of mountains, rivers, forests, wildlife, valleys, roads, villages, architecture, food, people, traditions, and experiences—not reduced to a single cultural stereotype.
- Copy should be concise, specific, and human rather than generic travel marketing language.
- The current Photographic direction uses photography as interface, narrative, and visual rhythm. It must avoid generic gallery, SaaS, and AI-template conventions.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing structured destination, experience, vehicle, and journey data under `src/data/`.
- Existing authentic Northeast India photography under `public/`, including Dawki, Kaziranga, Tawang, Majuli, Meghalaya waterfalls, living root bridges, Sikkim landscapes, and vehicle cutouts.
- Existing working routes, booking/enquiry flows, theme controls, and navigation.
- No approved testimonials, ratings, awards, customer metrics, or unsupported business claims are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Let real places and useful travel choices lead the story.
2. Make vehicle booking and complete-trip planning equally clear and credible.
3. Earn trust through specificity, coherent UX, authentic imagery, and professional execution.
4. Use visual expression and motion to improve discovery and continuity, never to obscure content or interaction.
5. Preserve product truth and existing functionality while the landing-page direction is evaluated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, meaningful alternative text, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion behavior across mobile and desktop.
